### THE PROBLEM

**Heading:** The industry runs on phone calls, spreadsheets and guesswork.

**Pain 1 — Processors**  
Kill slots get booked weeks out, then cancelled. Compliance docs arrive late or not at all. You're chasing suppliers the morning stock is due on the floor.

**Pain 2 — Feedlots & Producers**  
You don't know which processor is paying the best grid this week. You're guessing dressing percentages, negotiating blind, and booking on gut feel.

**Pain 3 — Everyone**  
There's no single system. Every relationship runs on a different spreadsheet, a different email thread, a different phone number saved in someone's mobile.

---

### HOW IT WORKS

**Heading:** One platform. The whole chain.

**Step 1 — Processors set their grids and capacity**  
Kill schedules, grid pricing, species weights, compliance requirements — all live, all in one place. Buyers and their feedlot clients can see exactly what's available when.

**Step 2 — Feedlots and producers match their stock**  
Express interest in kill slots, compare net return per head across processors, track feed costs and target exit weights in real time.

**Step 3 — Stock moves with full documentation**  
PIC numbers, eNVD references and compliance checks flow automatically. No last-minute calls. No missing paperwork. No surprises at the gate.
